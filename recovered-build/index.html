PRODUCT BOUNDARY

AccessLayer reports what the policy says, what it leaves unclear, and what to ask next.

It does not decide whether an event is legally compliant. Every documented finding points back to the source passage. Missing information stays missing instead of being filled in by the model.

Read the policy

Paste the event policy or load the synthetic fixture. AccessLayer extracts access commitments and keeps the source text attached.

Map the gaps

Each topic is marked documented, partial, ambiguous, or missing. “Not documented” never becomes “not available.”

Request less data

Participants describe the support they need. Diagnosis and medical history are not requested.

Keep the record

Request state, source excerpts, organizer actions, and unresolved gaps can be exported for human follow-up.
